## 🎯 OBJECTIVE

Enhance Lab 14 calculator with:
- 4 additional operations (power, modulus, squareRoot, percentage)
- Input validation and error handling
- Calculation history (last 5 calculations)
- Clear history button

---

## 💡 NEW FEATURES

**Additional Operations:**
1. `power(base, exponent)` - Example: 2^3 = 8
2. `modulus(a, b)` - Example: 10 % 3 = 1
3. `squareRoot(num)` - Example: √16 = 4
4. `percentage(num, percent)` - Example: 20% of 50 = 10

**Validation:**
- Check for non-numeric inputs
- Prevent division by zero
- Prevent negative square root
- Handle empty inputs

**History:**
- Store last 5 calculations in array
- Display as \"5 + 3 = 8\"
- Clear history button
