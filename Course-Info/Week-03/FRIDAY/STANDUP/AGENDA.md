## 📋 DETAILED AGENDA

### 1. Welcome & Week 3 Recap (2:00-2:05)

**Instructor Opening:**
> "Welcome to our Week 3 standup! This week has been HUGE. You went from zero HTML/CSS knowledge to building responsive, beautiful web pages. Let's celebrate that! 🎉"

**Quick Recap:**
- ✅ Monday: Learned semantic HTML5
- ✅ Tuesday: Mastered the Box Model and CSS fundamentals
- ✅ Wednesday: Built flexible layouts with Flexbox
- ✅ Thursday: Made designs responsive across all devices

**Energy Check:**
> "On a scale of 1-10, how confident do you feel about HTML and CSS now? Shout out a number!"

---

### 2. Student Shares (2:05-2:20)

**Format:** Each student has 3 minutes max (use timer!)

**Three Questions for Each Student:**
1. **What did you accomplish this week?**
   - Labs? Tickets? Personal wins?
2. **What was your biggest 'aha!' moment?**
   - When did something click?
3. **What are you excited to learn next week?**
   - JavaScript? DOM manipulation?

**Instructor Role:**
- ✅ Celebrate every win (applause, high-fives!)
- ✅ Ask follow-up questions to draw out details
- ✅ Note blockers quickly (address after standup or in office hours)
- ✅ Keep time (friendly but firm)

**Student Order:**
- Go around the circle (or randomize with name picker)
- Encourage specific examples: "I built a navigation bar with Flexbox that works on mobile!" vs. "I did some coding"

---

### 3. Week 4 Preview (2:20-2:25)

**Instructor Hype:**
> "Next week, we're adding SUPERPOWERS to your websites. You're going to make things MOVE, CHANGE, and RESPOND to users. We're diving into JavaScript!"

**Week 4 Topics Teaser:**
- **Monday:** Advanced CSS - animations, transitions, making things beautiful
- **Tuesday:** JavaScript fundamentals - making pages interactive
- **Wednesday:** DOM manipulation - changing pages on the fly
- **Thursday:** Forms & LocalStorage - saving data like a pro

**Big Reveal:**
> "And on Friday... we're kicking off Sprint 02! You're building a professional portfolio website. This is what you'll show employers. No pressure, but this is a BIG DEAL. 🚀"

**Show Example:**
- Pull up a beautiful portfolio website
- "By end of Week 6, you'll have something like this!"

---

### 4. Weekend Challenge & Closing (2:25-2:30)

**Optional Weekend Challenge:**
> "If you want to get ahead, here's a fun weekend challenge: Build a one-page website about your favorite hobby. Use everything you learned this week - semantic HTML, CSS styling, Flexbox layout, and make it responsive. Share it in Discord on Monday if you do it!"

**Reminders:**
- ✅ **Tickets Due:**
  - Ticket 11: Monday Week 4
  - Ticket 12: Tuesday Week 4
- ✅ **Office Hours:** Available via Discord all weekend if you get stuck
- ✅ **Rest!** You earned it after an intense week

**Closing:**
> "I'm SO proud of what you accomplished this week. You're no longer beginners - you're developers who can build real websites. Enjoy your weekend, rest up, and come back Monday ready to add JavaScript magic to your skills! 🎉"

**Final Words:**
- Group high-five or fist bump circle
- "See you Monday at 2 PM!"

## 📝 INSTRUCTOR NOTES

### Things to Celebrate (Look for These)
- ✅ Students completing labs on time
- ✅ Helping each other in Discord
- ✅ "Aha!" moments with Flexbox or responsive design
- ✅ Creative solutions beyond lab requirements
- ✅ Persistence through debugging

### Common Blockers to Address
- ❌ Still struggling with Flexbox (point to Flexbox Froggy game, office hours)
- ❌ Ticket submission confusion (review GitHub process)
- ❌ Feeling behind (normalize, offer catch-up plan)

### Energy Management
- Keep it SHORT (30 min max)
- High energy, lots of praise
- Don't let one student dominate (use timer)
- End on a high note (Week 4 excitement)
